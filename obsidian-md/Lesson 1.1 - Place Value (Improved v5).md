## Block 5 — Practice Lab: P4 (changed)

### ✏️v5 P4 — "Wrong direction" (MK 2004 Q13 concept, original framing)

**Type:** MCQ | **Difficulty:** 4pt | **Source:** original

**Question:** Benny was supposed to move his number one step LEFT on the place value chart. By mistake, he moved it one step RIGHT instead. He ended up with 40. What was the correct answer supposed to be?

**Options:** (A) 4 · (B) 400 · **(C) 4,000** ✓ · (D) 40,000 · (E) 400,000

**Explanation:** Moving right divides by 10. He got 40, so his number was 40 times 10 = 400. Moving left multiplies by 10. 400 times 10 = 4,000.

| Hint | Text | Words |
|------|------|-------|
| H1 | He went the wrong way. He should go left. | 9 |
| H2 | Going right made his number smaller. He got 40. | 9 |
| H3 | Find his real number first. Then move it left. | 9 |

> ✏️v5 **Why this beats the v4 P4:** "Digit 6 in 3,600 moves left" tests the rule directly but passively — the student just applies ×10 once. The Benny question requires 2 steps (reverse the wrong move to recover the original, then apply the correct move) and has a narrative hook (someone made a mistake). The same ×10 concept, deeper reasoning, more engaging story.
> All hints ≤10 words. H3 points toward the method without naming 4,000.

## Block 6 — Mastery Check: MC4 (changed)

### ✏️v5 MC4 — "Read and name" (zero-placeholder)

**Type:** MCQ | **Difficulty:** 3pt | **Source:** original | **No hints**

**Question:** A number has 5 Hundreds, 0 Tens, and 8 Ones. What is the number?

**Options:** (A) 58 · **(B) 508** ✓ · (C) 580 · (D) 5,008 · (E) 5,080

**Explanation:** 5 Hundreds = 500. 0 Tens = 0. 8 Ones = 8. Total: 500 + 0 + 8 = 508.

> ✏️v5 **Why this is better than v4 MC4:** The v4 MC4 tested "74 minus (7 × 7)" which is pure arithmetic — nothing to do with place value. The lesson's three tools are: (1) break apart, (2) build biggest/smallest, (3) read and name. MC1–MC3 all test Tool 2. MC4 must test Tool 3. This question tests Tool 3 with a zero-placeholder (the hardest sub-concept of the lesson) — if a student forgets the 0, they write 58 or 580. Each distractor maps to a specific misconception.

**Distractor analysis:**
| Option | What it represents |
|--------|-------------------|
| 58 | Dropped the zero entirely |
| 508 ✓ | Correct |
| 580 | Put 8 in Tens and 0 in Ones instead |
| 5,008 | Misread Hundreds as Thousands |
| 5,080 | Misread Hundreds as Thousands + dropped the 8 |
